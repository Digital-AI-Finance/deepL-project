The approval chain

Drafting is the cheap step. Review is the constraint. Automating drafting while asking for three to five times more variants multiplies load on the bottleneck.

The ratio ranges from 1.396 to 1.4. The spread is the finding.

A negative result worth keeping: this model was built expecting tiering to help most where queue time dominates. It does not. Elapsed time depends only on queue plus service per role, because waiting and working add the same way in series and in parallel alike. The split earns its place in capacity planning, not in cycle time.

What would be needed to turn this into a number

  • hours an asset waits before that reviewer starts, by role (queue time)

  • reviewer hours available per month, by role
  • the current mix of derivative versus new versus regulated content
